Equipment and Safety

You don't have to buy everything to start. This page shows what you need for a match, what you can share at practice, and what to check with PE.

Formal match kit

  • Shirt, shorts and socks
  • Shin guards and footwear

Shared practice gear

  • Ball, cones and bibs
  • Ask what school can provide

Check with PE

  • Surface and footwear
  • School rules and supervision
  • Warm up and bring water

Check what your school needs before you play or buy kit. Goalkeeper gloves are optional. They are not on the formal equipment list.

Note for PE teachers: please check that this advice matches our school rules before the site is shared with students.
