Make room for a new teammate

Joining a new game can feel scary. You can help a new player feel welcome with these three easy actions.

Invite someone

Ask without pressure. "Do you want to play with us?" is enough.

Explain one rule

Don't explain everything at once. Pick one rule and show an example.

Share the play

Pass to the new player and give everyone a turn, not just the best players.

See a sample invitation

"Hi! We play football at lunch break. Do you want to join us? You don't need to be good. We will pass to you and help you learn."
