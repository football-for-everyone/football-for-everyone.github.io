Start playing one step at a time

New to football? That's okay! Follow these three steps, then try the quick quiz.

1. Prepare

Wear trainers and comfy clothes. Bring water. Ask a PE teacher when there is a game or club you can join.

2. Try a pass

Stand a few big steps away from a partner. Pass the ball with the inside of your foot. Count how many passes you can do in a row.

3. Check and act

See what kit you need on Equipment and Safety. Then choose your first step on Take Action.
